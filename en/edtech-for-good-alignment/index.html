WHAT SVEED IS

An independent review app that helps primary and lower secondary school children practise what has already been taught in the classroom. It works offline, follows Brazil's BNCC curriculum, and includes accessibility and inclusion features.

Method, scope, and limitations of this mapping

This mapping covers the 30 criteria in Layer C, which comprises the five pillars, and does not cover the framework's other organizational and product requirements. A technical element indicates an implementation related to part of a criterion; a document or informal experience still requires operational evidence. The counts are descriptive and do not represent an official score.

SW-8 and TL-5 do not apply to the stated scope because the app does not use AI. SW-9 remains relevant to rule-based automated decisions. Pilots, specialist educational review, and audits are still required. The partnership must produce evidence capable of confirming, limiting, or revising the product's assumptions. This interpretation remains subject to external assessors.
